Precision Diesel Pump & Injector Calibration

Zalven Calibration tests, repairs, and calibrates inline and rotary diesel fuel injection pumps and injectors for cars, trucks, and heavy equipment, backed by genuine OEM parts and bench-tested accuracy. The shop is located in Caloocan City, Metro Manila, and serves walk-in and scheduled customers from across the surrounding area.

Diesel Fuel System Services

From a single injector to a full inline pump rebuild, every job leaves the bench calibrated and verified.

  • Fuel Pump Calibration — bench-tested calibration of inline and rotary diesel fuel injection pumps for accurate fuel delivery and timing.
  • Injector Testing & Repair — nozzle cleaning, spray-pattern testing, and injector overhaul to restore proper atomization and pressure.
  • Pump Overhaul & Rebuild — full teardown, inspection, and rebuild of mechanical fuel pump assemblies using genuine OEM components.
  • Common Rail Diagnostics — precision diagnostics and calibration for modern common-rail diesel injection systems.
  • Heavy Equipment Service — on-site and in-shop fuel system service for trucks, tractors, generators, and heavy machinery.
  • Genuine Parts & Rebuild Kits — replacement using trusted-brand parts to keep every pump and injector performing to spec.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is diesel fuel injection pump calibration?
It's precision testing and adjustment of a diesel engine's fuel injection pump and injectors on a calibration test bench, to restore correct fuel delivery, timing, and spray pattern. Proper calibration affects engine power, fuel economy, smoke, and starting performance.

What engine and vehicle brands does Zalven Calibration service?
Isuzu, Mitsubishi, Toyota, Caterpillar, Perkins, Volvo, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai, and Stanadyne diesel engines, covering cars, trucks, and heavy equipment.

Do you service heavy equipment, not just vehicles?
Yes. In addition to cars and trucks, the shop services diesel fuel injection pumps and injectors on heavy equipment such as generators, tractors, and other machinery, both in-shop and on-site.

What parts do you use for repairs and rebuilds?
Genuine parts from trusted brands including Bosch, Delphi, Zexel, OMS, and Interstate-McBee.
